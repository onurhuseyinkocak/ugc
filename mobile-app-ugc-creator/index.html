Questions brands ask

Have you made sponsored content for a mobile app? +

Not yet. My published paid partnerships are Brantial and Mostdomain, both web products. I build my own iPhone app, Promtable, so I can demonstrate an app workflow from the builder’s side.

What should a mobile app brief include? +

The target user, one workflow to demonstrate, device and account requirements, the claims the brand can substantiate, the launch or deadline date and the platforms where the content will run.

Which format suits an app launch? +

A Reel suits a single workflow shown on screen; a carousel suits a step-by-step explanation people save. One Reel is $300 USD and one sponsored carousel is $100 USD. Length, slide count, language, revisions and publication channel are fixed in the written brief. Paid ads, whitelisting and extra usage are licensed separately.

Do you guarantee installs or conversions? +

No. Sponsored content is disclosed, and views, installs, sales, rankings and conversions are not guaranteed. I publish dated measurements for the work instead.
