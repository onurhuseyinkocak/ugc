Questions brands ask

What does a tech UGC creator do for an AI or SaaS product? +

I use the product, choose one workflow a buyer cares about and explain it on camera with a screen demo, as a vertical Reel or a carousel. The Brantial Reel is a published example: an AI-search visibility product explained as a walkthrough.

How much does a Reel for an AI tool cost? +

One Reel is $300 USD and one sponsored carousel is $100 USD. Length, slide count, language, revisions and publication channel are fixed in the written brief. Paid ads, whitelisting and extra usage are licensed separately.

Can you show results from earlier SaaS work? +

Yes. The Brantial Reel has 7,108 views, 5,324 accounts reached and 138 saves; the Mostdomain Reel has 6,086 views and 2,605 accounts reached (lifetime, Meta Graph API, measured 16 Nov 2026). These are post measurements, not sales or conversion figures, and no result is guaranteed.

Which languages do you create in? +

Turkish and English. The sponsored Reels published so far are in Turkish.

What do you need to start? +

Product access that is sufficient to demonstrate the chosen workflow, the target audience, the claims the brand can substantiate, the deadline and the intended platforms.
